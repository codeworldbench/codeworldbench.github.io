#How to read these numbers

GEN and category columns are macro-averages

The mean of per-environment case pass rates — passed cases over total cases, computed inside each environment, then averaged across environments. Every environment carries equal weight regardless of how many cases it holds.

P2P and F2P are micro-averages

Case counts are pooled across environments. P2P divides still-passing by initially passing cases; F2P divides newly passing by initially failing cases. Every case carries equal weight.

Why the leaderboard shows GEN alone

Because GEN is a macro-average over environments, the all-118 score is the environment-weighted mean of the two subsets. P2P and F2P cannot be combined the same way without per-subset case counts, so they are reported per subset here.

#What the tables show

Construction is the bottleneck, and preservation hides it

P2P averages 96.5 on common and 94.0 on professional, with every row above 90 on common and all but one on professional. Mean F2P is 16.5 and 25.2; mean GEN sits between them at 50.7 and 51.9, over a 22-point spread in both pools.

Repair is not ordered by construction

The strongest professional F2P rows are Qwen3.8-27B under Qwen Code (45.3) and Claude Code (44.9), above every closed-source row, where Claude Opus 5 leads at 33.5. Those rows rank twelfth and seventh on GEN. Not a family effect: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B repairs at 8.6–10.7.

The model moves a score more than the scaffold

Professional GEN spread across scaffolds is 2.0 points for Qwen3.8-27B and 3.0 for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (5.5 and 5.1 on common), against a 14.8-point professional gap between the two families. Repair is the scaffold-sensitive half: professional F2P for Qwen3.8-27B ranges 29.2 to 45.3.

Category strength is not overall ranking

Across the 14 default-scaffold models, common scores average 66.7 in Geospatial Construction and 32.2 in Manufacturing Agriculture; professional averages are 71.5 in Supply Operations and 28.4 in Enterprise Operations. MiniMax-M3 leads Enterprise Operations (39.75) ahead of GPT-6 Astra (24.26) despite lower overall GEN (44.96 vs. 60.36).
